WHAT WE HANDLE

Legal Services & Practice Areas

Adv. Nikhil Shakarwal provides focused legal guidance and representation with careful preparation, clear communication and personal attention to each matter.

01 · FAMILY LAW

Divorce Law

Legal assistance in mutual and contested divorce matters, including preparation of petitions, documentation, settlement discussions and representation before the appropriate court.

02 · FAMILY LAW

Domestic Violence

Guidance and representation in matters involving domestic abuse, protection proceedings, residence concerns, maintenance and other remedies available under applicable law.

03 · MATRIMONIAL

Matrimonial Law

Legal support for matrimonial disputes including separation, maintenance, marital rights, settlement and related proceedings, with an emphasis on practical resolution.

04 · FAMILY LAW

Family Disputes

Assistance in family-related disputes involving custody, maintenance, matrimonial issues and other proceedings, with attention to the interests of all concerned.

05 · CRIMINAL LAW

Bail Matters

Legal assistance for regular and anticipatory bail proceedings, including preparation of applications, supporting documents and representation before the concerned court.

06 · FINANCIAL DISPUTES

Cheque Bounce

Assistance in cheque dishonour matters, including legal notices, complaint proceedings, defence strategy and representation in proceedings under applicable law.

07 · CIVIL LAW

Civil Law

Representation and legal guidance in civil disputes involving contracts, recovery, injunctions, property-related claims and other civil proceedings.

08 · CRIMINAL LAW

Criminal Law

Defence and representation in criminal matters, including FIR-related proceedings, complaints, trial proceedings and other criminal litigation before the appropriate courts.

09 · APPELLATE MATTERS

Supreme Court Matters

Legal assistance in matters requiring proceedings before the Supreme Court, including case preparation, documentation and coordination for appropriate representation.

10 · BUSINESS LAW

Corporate Law

Legal support for businesses in agreements, documentation, commercial disputes, compliance-related matters and other corporate legal requirements.
