MISCELLANEOUS LEGAL WORK

Court & Authority Matters

We assist clients with legal work that may arise before courts, government departments, statutory authorities, tribunals and other public bodies. The scope may include preparing applications and representations, reviewing notices and orders, coordinating required documentation and assisting with proceedings before the appropriate authority.

Each matter is considered according to the relevant authority, applicable procedure and documents involved. Clients can receive guidance on the steps required, the documents to be prepared and the appropriate forum or department for pursuing the matter.

Areas of Assistance

  • Miscellaneous applications and proceedings before courts
  • Matters and representations before DDA
  • Matters relating to MCD and municipal authorities
  • Income Tax Department notices, representations and related proceedings
  • Assistance before tribunals and statutory authorities
  • Applications, replies, representations and supporting documentation
  • Review of notices, orders and official communications
  • Coordination and procedural assistance in authority-related matters

Assistance Before Courts & Authorities

Where a matter involves a government department, tribunal or other authority, timely review of the notice, order and supporting documents can help identify the appropriate procedural steps. Legal assistance can be provided based on the facts and the jurisdiction of the concerned authority.
